# Task19 handoff: integrate preparation and replace ReduceAsync

Contract: [Task19](https://github.com/microsoft/typespec/issues/12454#issuecomment-6543557177).
Dependencies: accepted capabilities from Tasks01-18. Apply the
[shared guide](./pre-emission-implementation-guide.md).

## Scope and non-alternative architecture

Compose supported type, receiver, default, grouping, attribute, conditional and
documentation decisions into one occurrence/file preparation plan. Resolve
parent/child spellings, original/surviving imports, source observations and grammar
before one writer consumes the choices.

The finished replacement must NEVER invoke general `Simplifier.ReduceAsync` to
decide reductions, including opaque/unknown/custom-rewriter cases. A finite
post-write semantic reducer, second generated-text traversal or per-family owner
is not an alternative. Preserve normal formatting/customization contracts.

Input/admission assessment, ordinary Node/default activation, broader SDK adoption
or regeneration and performance campaigns remain explicit separate work. Do not
recreate the withdrawn owned-validation/MSBuild/Csc/publication product, broaden
input admission by inertia or remove existing guards.

## Start with a finite interaction ledger

Carry forward every accepted original positive and retention anchor. Map
interactions that actually share dependencies: type/grouping receiver tokens,
source capture and layout, default/type/documentation import witnesses, conditional
ownership and late input/policy invalidation. Do not build an arbitrary language
cross-product or an independent evaluator for every family.

A useful early checkpoint composes an already adopted type/default/documentation
interaction with its retained witness counterexample. Do not invent missing
upstream capabilities as untracked integration work; assign the bounded gap to
its owning task. Incremental draft integration is allowed.

## Shared composition, not duplicated engines

- Reuse canonical provider/IR relationships, existing source/reference symbols,
  catalogs, lookup/source/grammar facts and actual-use receipts.
- Make dependency direction explicit. Original-use facts precede selected uses;
  surviving witnesses reflect the chosen type/default/reference plans. Prevent
  circular import authorization rather than introducing a general diagnostic or
  search solver.
- Final-child spellings feed grouping/separators before emission. A parent lookup
  retention is not a blanket child veto; source/role exclusion is.
- Keep freshness sticky. Validate shared inputs and affected permissions at the
  required boundaries, including after callbacks/residual-input operations.
  Preserve independent field/body/sibling choices where their contracts allow.
- Emit each callback/output graph once. Late failure uses captured qualified
  alternatives, not replay, generated-output rebinding or a new rule-engine pass.
- Preserve diagnostics including pre-existing errors and mapped paths, original
  trivia/directives/visibility and observed caller text/line values.

## First real integration checkpoint

Use actual standard generation and admitted input/owner state, not only direct
producer APIs. Combine at least the selected families whose dependency interaction
is being claimed. Verify final raw names/forms, native targets/constructions/
conversions/constants and exact witness/capture controls. Include real field/body
coexistence when the adopted profile permits it; synthetic body appends prove
mechanics, not production admission.

Test late physical-input, import, provider, source-policy and rewriter changes.
Prove sticky failure and callback count one. Unsupported original cases retain
their accepted representation without contaminating unrelated safe decisions.

## Retire legacy ownership only after coverage

During migration, the existing residual path may serve families not yet adopted.
It is scaffolding, not completion. Track which families/files still depend on it;
a raw reduction count does not establish that simplifier cost was removed.

Before declaring the complete replacement done:

1. All accepted Tasks01-18 positives/retentions and their required combinations
   have prepared and raw/pipeline evidence. No dropped/re-grounded or silently
   relabeled unsupported anchor.
2. Tests prove zero calls to general ReduceAsync on the replacement path,
   including opaque/custom cases, and no per-family post-write substitute.
3. One-write/canonical occurrence, deterministic output, input/policy/freshness and
   actual consumer-contract claims are validated.
4. Relevant full generator/integration checks and independent review pass, with
   outstanding required CI/SDK gates disclosed. Archived product-specific tests
   remain historical evidence, not resurrected current gates.
5. Native serialized/reparsed output preserves applicable API/reflection/
   serialization/XML/source observations, not only compilability.

## Final output policy and stops

Readable SDK output must not use `global::` as a permanent escape hatch. If a
concrete retained-source case conflicts with the desired final spelling, bring
the original expectation, actual consumer and bounded options for an explicit
policy decision. Do not silently change captured text, introduce a source-equivalence engine or assume ordinary namespace-qualified spelling is approved.

Do not solve a missing accepted case with a new compiler host, broad rule expansion,
weaker diagnostic policy or selective public rollout. Complexity/allocation cleanup
and profiling do not waive correctness. Broader/default enablement requires its
own authorization and cannot be claimed while the input contract or required
checks remain unresolved.

Anchors: the complete accepted Structural production/integration inventory,
current PreparedFieldTypeTests/PreparedFieldTypeClientTests and affected provider,
writer, reference-map, capture and residual tests. Scope completion is the
coordinated replacement above, not a claim that all SDK activation work is done.
